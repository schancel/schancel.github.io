What this experiment establishes

Runs compiled C++ directly; there is no JavaScript agreement algorithm. Eight fixed eligible participants each own a synthetic mempool. Confirmed rates govern new admission and wallet quotes, while existing accepted transactions remain. Local desired rate starts from the operator seed, or the network-confirmed seed when operator seed is zero. Working proposals aggregate gossip; desired rates do not enforce directly.

Resources are synthetic CPU, write, queue, and growth models, not measured I/O or real CPU benchmarks. Cheap invalid traffic needs handling outside fees. This model does not implement authenticated Avalanche participation, full relay, full validation, signatures, or a block-consensus minimum fee. The shared activation ledger assumes a common synthetic chain; it is not a demonstrated distributed safety protocol. Proposal-network partitions and voter outages affect fee agreement only: transaction links remain connected, so payments test the held confirmed fee. No transaction-network partition or reorganization is simulated. Per-link deterministic added delay from zero to the configured jitter, above base latency, supports asymmetric and stale-report experiments. Retained transactions can still mine below a later confirmed admission fee; that fee is not a block-consensus rule.

Payment policy: the sender chooses gross, the wallet automatically bounds and deducts a fee, and the recipient sees gross, fee, and net. Invoice maximum-fee and minimum-net limits have quiet defaults and clear deferral reasons. These research controls are not routine wallet prompts. There are no auctions, refunds, asset-price forecasts, or supply assumptions.
